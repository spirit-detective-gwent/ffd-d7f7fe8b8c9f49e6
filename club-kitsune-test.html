Private page. Drag to walk, tap the floor to go there, double-tap or Shift to run. Walk up to a machine or a table — it glows. Tap it, or the round button. Suit mode stands in for Jin’s suit.

Before the suit, a win is +25 and a loss is +5. A Suit inside the ring while you are playing fills the meter. Suit mode: a win is +5, and a Suit nearby adds about +4 a second. The bartender still asks for ID.

Ryo has no sit sheet, so his seat, pull, pump and slump are his walk sprite posed on the stool. Guests who are sitting use the game’s real sit sheets. Their lever pull is a lean on that seated sprite.
