A. Aoi's current sounds (from the game, v4.30.1)

1. COUNTER (her guard's counter fires)

When an enemy attacks into Aoi's counter guard, "COUNTER −n" shows and the game plays clash, plus the enemy's hurt voice 0.12 s later.

2. When an enemy HITS Aoi

The enemy cards have no animation clip, so the game uses its fallback: a whoosh swing, then 0.3 s later a hit (picked at random from hit / hit_b / hit_c, never the same twice in a row). Then Aoi's hurt voice: one of hurt_f / hurt_f_b / hurt_f_c at random (volume 0.8, 0.05 s after the hit), once per attack. If her guard soaks part of the hit, guard plays too ("BLOCK n"); if it soaks all of it, only guard plays and there is no hurt voice. The enemy also shouts on attacking, but that is the enemy's own voice. There is no fist or kick sound on enemy hits; those keys are Ryo's.

3. When Aoi plays a GUARD card

Every guard-type card plays pl_guard the moment it is played. Her counter cards (Kote Return, Parry Counter, Club Riposte) are guard cards with a counter value, so they play the same pl_guard. But each card's little animation adds its own sounds afterwards, and that is where they differ:

  • Kote Return, Parry Counter (the parry clip): whoosh, her grunt (55% of the time), then clash about 1.1 s in. So you hear the clash when she plays these cards and again when the counter fires.
  • Club Riposte: whoosh, her grunt (40%), then guard.
  • Upper Guard Kamae: buff. Wind Step, Mirage Step: charge + whoosh.
  • In a combo the clip sounds are skipped; only the combo sound plays.
