Short, punchy win sounds in the same spirit as the current K.O.! jingle: about 0.5 to 1.5 s, a quick flourish or a hit, no long fanfares and no 8-bit/chiptune. All are real recordings or composed music (no AI, no TTS, nothing generated by code), and all allow commercial use: CC0 needs no credit, CC BY needs a credit line (see CREDITS.md).

Item 0 (gold border) is what the game plays now, for comparison. Items 1 to 7 come from the same Kenney pack as the current sound, so they will blend in most easily.

The ▶ Play, then victory song button plays the sting, then the first ~10 s of "Lively Meadow" (Matthew Pablo, CC BY 3.0, from the victory song page). Tap it again to stop.

Levels: silence trimmed, loudness lightly matched to the current K.O. sound (peaks at or below -1 dB). Nobody has listened to these yet: the durations, note shapes and attack counts were measured from the audio, and the descriptions come from that plus the authors' notes.
