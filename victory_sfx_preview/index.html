🦊🔥 Battle-win sound candidates

These are short sounds (0.5 to 4.6 s) for the instant you win a card battle, before the victory song starts. They are real recordings or composed music (no AI, no TTS, nothing generated by code), and every one allows commercial use: CC0 needs no credit, and CC BY needs a credit line. The top three (gold border) are what the game plays now, for comparison.

The ▶ Play, then victory song button plays the sound, then the first ~10 s of "Lively Meadow" (Matthew Pablo, CC BY 3.0, from the victory song page). It is only there so you can hear the pair together. Tap it again to stop.

Levels: silence trimmed, long tails faded, loudness lightly matched (peaks around -1 dB). Nobody has listened to these yet. The descriptions come from the authors' notes and the waveforms.

NOW IN GAME: story-mode win hit (taiko.mp3)

Scott Harding, "Taiko" (live recording, Singapore Wind Symphony percussion) · CC0 · 2.0 s

Plays the moment you land the final blow in a story fight (hit-stop and sparks). The game also plays its own punch and grunt, slowed down, at the same time. It is a cluster of live taiko drum hits with a short roll.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:02_Taiko2_(short).oga (Wikimedia Commons)

NOW IN GAME: quick-duel "K.O.!" win (roundwin.mp3)

Kenney, Music Jingles (jingles_SAX10) · CC0 · 1.0 s

Plays with the K.O.! banner in non-story duels. It is a short saxophone jingle, about 1 s long.

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles (Kenney)

NOW IN GAME: grade slam (gong.mp3), plays later

the_very_Real_Horst (Chinese Tao-Chi gong) · CC0 · 3.0 s

For reference only: in story mode this plays when the GRADE letter slams on, just before the victory music starts. It is not the first win hit.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:240382_the-very-real-horst_chinese-gong-finish-session-2014-06-10-29-143.wav (Wikimedia Commons)
