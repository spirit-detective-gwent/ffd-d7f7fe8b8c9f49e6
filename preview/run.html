Notes

  • From your clips (as described to me): very low forward lean, smooth glide with a small bob, hard shoulder pivot, elbows bent about 90° with tight fists, the back elbow driven high, the forward fist at head height, long strides landing on the forefoot, the back foot kicked up high, 12 frames per two-step cycle, horizontal speed lines and a little dust where the foot lands.
  • My own design, not copied from the clips: the exact leg and arm curves (the timing tables in tools/v426/sprint.js), the gakuran tails flying back over his back with a two-speed flutter (2 waves and 3 ripples per cycle, modelled on the monks' shirts), the faint air streak off the tail, and the front and back views of the tails (they flare at the hips from the front and rise over his back from behind).
  • Cadence: the brief asked for both "12 frames per two-step cycle at 24 fps" (4 steps a second) and "about 8 taps a second". Those two don't agree. In the game the cycle is locked to the planted foot, so there's no foot slide: 12 frames per 102 px, which at run speed comes to 4.5 taps/s (27 fps). "8 taps/s" here is double time for comparison: the art plays at 48 fps and the feet slide. The cadence buttons change only the new run. The old run always plays at its v4.29 game speed. Tell me which one you want.
  • Sounds: real recordings, CC0, by Joseph SARDIN at BigSoundBank. Hard ground: #0514 "Footsteps, Shoe on Concrete" (a man running in sneakers on concrete). Grass: #1843 "Running in the Tall Grass". I picked four single taps from each and gave them short fades. On gravel the run keeps the existing gravel crunch.
  • Not verified: I can't watch video or hear audio. The feel of the motion and the tap timing and sound are checked only by frame stills and by tests (taps land on contact frames 0 and 6, about 4.5 a second). Please judge them by eye and ear here.
